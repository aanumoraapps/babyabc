BabyAbc

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Android app: com.mora.babyabc

This policy explains how BabyAbc handles information when children and families use its alphabet, tracing, word and number learning activities.

Learning stays on your device. The current app works offline, does not require an account, and does not include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not collect or transmit personal information through the app.

Information stored on your device

BabyAbc saves completed learning activities and your preferences for language, spoken instructions, sound effects and haptic feedback in the app's private local storage. This helps the app remember progress and settings. These records are not sent to us or synced to a developer-operated server.

Information we do not collect

The app does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth or account details. It does not access location, contacts, photos, the camera or microphone. It does not collect advertising identifiers, record children's voices, or send usage or crash reports to us.

Speech, sounds and device services

Spoken letters, words, numbers and instructions use Android's text-to-speech service. The app selects an available voice that does not require a network connection and passes learning phrases to that service. Speech may be unavailable if a suitable offline voice is not installed. Installing voice packs and the operation of your chosen system speech provider are controlled by your device settings and that provider's policies. BabyAbc does not record audio. Sound effects are bundled with the app.

Children's privacy

BabyAbc is designed for children with parent or guardian involvement. We do not collect personal information from children through the app. There are no accounts, chat features, advertising or behavioral tracking. Parents can manage learning preferences and reset progress in the app's parent settings.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share personal information collected through BabyAbc because the app does not collect such information. There are no advertising or analytics partners integrated into the current app.

Retention, deletion and controls

Local progress and preferences remain on the device until removed. Resetting progress in parent settings deletes completed-activity records but preserves preferences. To remove all local app data, use Android Settings to clear BabyAbc's app storage or uninstall the app. No account deletion request is needed because the app has no accounts and we do not hold a server copy of learning records.

Security

Progress and settings use Android's app-private storage. The app does not operate a backend or cloud synchronization service. Device security also depends on your operating system and device settings.

This privacy-policy website

This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, forms or embedded third-party media. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes technical information associated with visits, including IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. Visiting this website is separate from using the offline Android app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its information practices change. The date above will reflect the latest revision. Any future data-collecting feature will need an updated disclosure before it is introduced.
