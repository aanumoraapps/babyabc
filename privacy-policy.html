BabyAbc

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Android app: com.mora.babyabc

This policy explains how BabyAbc handles information when children and families use its English and Hindi letter tracing, letter recognition, pair and picture matching, word building, alphabet and number puzzles, color and shape matching, counting, animal sounds, picture puzzles and sorting activities.

Learning stays on your device. The current app works offline, does not require an account, and does not include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not collect or transmit personal information through the app.

Information stored on your device

BabyAbc saves completed learning activities and achievements, instruction-language preferences, the Home English/Hindi alphabet choice, individual games' alphabet choices, and preferences for spoken instructions, sound effects and haptic feedback in the app's private local storage. These settings may remain after you close and reopen the app. Changing the alphabet choice does not delete achievements. These records are not sent to us or synced to a developer-operated server. Touches and drag movements are processed on the device to operate the games; they are not sent to us as behavioral analytics.

Information we do not collect

The app does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth or account details. It does not access location, contacts, photos, the camera or microphone. It does not collect advertising identifiers, record children's voices, or send usage or crash reports to us.

Speech, sounds and device services

Spoken letters, words, numbers and instructions use Android's text-to-speech service. The app selects an available voice that does not require a network connection and passes learning phrases to that service. Speech may be unavailable if a suitable offline voice is not installed. Installing voice packs and the operation of your chosen system speech provider are controlled by your device settings and that provider's policies. BabyAbc does not record audio. Sound effects and animal sounds are bundled with the app. English and Hindi learning phrases use the same offline voice-selection approach.

Children's privacy

BabyAbc is designed for children with parent or guardian involvement. We do not collect personal information from children through the app. There are no accounts, chat features, advertising or behavioral tracking. Parent settings and alphabet switches use an on-device arithmetic gate to discourage accidental changes by children. The gate does not ask for a parent's identity, age or contact details and is not identity or age verification.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share personal information collected through BabyAbc because the app does not collect such information. There are no advertising or analytics partners integrated into the current app.

Retention, deletion and controls

Local progress and preferences remain on the device until removed. The current version does not provide an in-app progress-reset button. To remove all local app data, use Android Settings to clear BabyAbc's app storage or uninstall the app. No account deletion request is needed because the app has no accounts and we do not hold a server copy of learning records.

Security

Progress and settings use Android's app-private storage. The app does not operate a backend or cloud synchronization service. Device security also depends on your operating system and device settings.

Reading this policy in the app

The in-app policy is bundled with BabyAbc and displayed in a local WebView that blocks network loading, JavaScript, and DOM storage. Reading it does not contact the policy website. If a parent chooses an external privacy link or the contact email link, the app opens a separate browser or email app; that service's privacy practices then apply.

This privacy-policy website

This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, forms or embedded third-party media. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes technical information associated with visits, including IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. Visiting this website is separate from using the offline Android app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its information practices change. The date above will reflect the latest revision. Any future data-collecting feature will need an updated disclosure before it is introduced.
